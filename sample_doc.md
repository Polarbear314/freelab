# A Short Multi-Topic Reference

## Coffee brewing

The flavor of brewed coffee depends heavily on grind size, water temperature,
and contact time. A coarse grind suits immersion methods like the French press,
where grounds steep in water for around four minutes before being separated by a
mesh plunger. A finer grind suits pour-over methods, where water passes through a
bed of coffee in a paper filter in two to three minutes. Water that is too hot,
above roughly 96 degrees Celsius, tends to scorch the grounds and produce bitter
flavors, while water below about 90 degrees under-extracts and tastes sour and
weak. Freshly roasted beans release carbon dioxide, so many baristas let a small
amount of water sit on the grounds for thirty seconds — the "bloom" — before
continuing to pour.

## The planets of the solar system

Our solar system has eight recognized planets orbiting the Sun. In order of
distance they are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and
Neptune. The four inner planets are small and rocky; the four outer planets are
gas or ice giants. Pluto was reclassified as a dwarf planet in 2006 by the
International Astronomical Union, which is why the count dropped from nine to
eight. Jupiter is the largest planet, with a mass greater than all the others
combined. The number of planets in the wider universe is unknown and effectively
uncountable, but within our own solar system the settled answer is eight.

## Storing olive oil

Olive oil degrades when exposed to heat, light, and air. To keep it fresh, store
it in a cool, dark cupboard rather than next to the stove, and keep the cap
tightly closed to limit oxidation. A dark glass or tin container protects against
light better than clear glass. Most extra-virgin olive oil is best used within a
year of pressing and within a few months of opening the bottle. Refrigeration is
not necessary and can cause the oil to turn cloudy, though this is harmless and
reverses at room temperature.

## Basic household electrical safety

Before working on an outlet or fixture, switch off the relevant circuit at the
breaker panel and confirm the power is off with a voltage tester. Water and
electricity are a dangerous combination, so never handle electrical devices with
wet hands or while standing on a damp floor. Extension cords are intended for
temporary use, not permanent wiring, and running them under rugs can trap heat
and create a fire risk. If a breaker trips repeatedly, that is a signal of an
underlying problem — an overloaded circuit or a fault — and should be inspected
rather than simply reset again and again.
